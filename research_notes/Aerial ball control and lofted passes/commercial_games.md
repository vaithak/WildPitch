# Aerial ball control and lofted passes in commercial football games

Scope: EA Sports FIFA / FC (era-marked), Konami PES / eFootball, older arcade titles (Sensible Soccer and MicroProse Soccer), and community feedback. Sources are labelled **[official]** (EA/Konami), **[community guide]** (fifplay, fifauteam, gamepressure, dexerto and similar) or **[forum]**. Some pages could not be fetched (help.ea.com timed out; EA forums returned 403; several older EA news URLs now redirect to FC 27 index pages). Where a claim rests only on a search-result snippet, it is marked *(snippet)*.

## Q1: Lofted/long pass and cross controls (power meter, variants, assistance)

### Takeaway
In FIFA/FC, the lob is one button (□/X). **Hold time sets power, which players use as distance.** Height is chosen mostly by **modifier buttons**, not by the meter: High lob is L1 + □, Driven lob is R1 + □ (FIFA 20) or L1 + R1 + □ (FC 26/27), Flair lob is L2 + □, and Low/ground cross is a double-tap of □. FC 26 also flattens lobs automatically when the path is clear. PES/eFootball uses one gauge that sets power. On shots that gauge also acts as height, and a separate "stunning/low lofted" variant gives a flatter, faster ball.

### Cited Findings
**EA FIFA / FC: button mapping by era**
- **FIFA 20 manual [community transcription of official manual]:** "Lob pass/ Cross/ Header: Square"; "Low cross: Square + Square"; "Driven lob pass/ Cross: R1 + Square"; "High lob/ Cross: L1 + Square"; "Flair lob: L2 + Square"; "Lobbed through pass: L1 + Triangle"; "Driven lobbed through pass: L1 + R1 + Triangle". Be a Pro also has "Call for ground cross: R1 + Square". — [fifauteam FIFA 20/21 manual](https://fifauteam.com/fifa-21-manual/)
- **FC 27 [community guide]:** Lob/Cross □; Precision Lob R1 + □; Lobbed Through L1 + △; High Lob L1 + □; Driven Lob **L1 + R1 + □**; Flair Lob L2 + □. Driven lob "travels faster and suits long diagonal switches"; too much power sends the ball "straight to the goalkeeper or out of play". — [fifplay FC 27 Lob Pass](https://www.fifplay.com/fc-27-lob-pass/)
- Era note: the driven lob moved from R1 + □ (FIFA 20) to L1 + R1 + □ (FC 26/27). In FC 24+, R1 became the "Precision Pass" modifier. — compare [fifauteam FIFA 20 manual](https://fifauteam.com/fifa-21-manual/) and [fifplay FC 27](https://www.fifplay.com/fc-27-lob-pass/); FC 24 introduced "the new Precision Pass" — [EA FC 24 Pitch Notes](https://www.ea.com/games/ea-sports-fc/pitch-notes/news/fc-24-gameplay-deepdive)
- **Header** shares the pass buttons in FIFA 20. Ground pass is ✕ = header pass along the ground, Lob is □ = lofted header, Shoot is ○ = headed shot, and L1 + R1 + ○ = downward header. Whether you get a header or a kick is decided by context (ball height), not by a separate button. — [fifauteam FIFA 20 manual](https://fifauteam.com/fifa-21-manual/)

**Power meter semantics (FIFA/FC)**
- Hold time sets distance: "same logic as ground passes … press for longer for more distance" (FIFA 21). — [Dexerto FIFA 21 passing guide](https://www.dexerto.com/fifa/the-ultimate-fifa-21-passing-crossing-gameplay-guide-1407583)
- Height comes from the variant. High Lob is "a lob pass with more height", and Flair Lob has "even more height than a high lob". — [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/fifa/the-ultimate-fifa-21-passing-crossing-gameplay-guide-1407583) *(snippet)*
- EA describes driven lobs as "faster, and they travel at a lower trajectory than regular Lob Passes". It also says players with higher Ball Control "will be able to control Driven Lob Passes more reliably". This text is from FIFA Mobile. — [EA Advanced Passing (FIFA Mobile)](https://ea.com/games/fifa/news/gameplay-advanced-passing) *(snippet; the page now redirects to an FC 27 index)*
- Assisted passing: power picks the **target receiver**. With 3 bars "the game thinks you want to pass the ball to a player further away" than with 1 bar. — anecdotal tutorial, cited via [search for FIFA 21](https://www.gamepressure.com/fifa-21/passes/zada05) *(snippet; this is player observation, not documented)*
- Power that is too weak gets intercepted; too strong goes out of play. — [Gamepressure FIFA 21](https://www.gamepressure.com/fifa-21/passes/zada05)
- Crosses: the "sweet spot" is 2–2.5 bars for a standard cross. — [magicgameworld FC 26 lob/cross tips](https://www.magicgameworld.com/ea-fc-26-lob-pass-and-cross-tips/) *(snippet, community)*
- I found no official EA formula for how bar length maps to lob height or distance. — [search summary](https://www.dexerto.com/fifa/the-ultimate-fifa-21-passing-crossing-gameplay-guide-1407583)

**Automatic trajectory shaping and tuning (official pitch notes)**
- FC 24: Lob and Lofted Through Passes became "more accurate, travel faster and are more difficult to intercept". This came with the Long Ball Pass PlayStyle, which FC 27 strengthened further. — [EA FC 24 Pitch Notes](https://www.ea.com/games/ea-sports-fc/pitch-notes/news/fc-24-gameplay-deepdive); [EA FC 27 PlayStyles](https://www.ea.com/cs/games/ea-sports-fc/fc-27/news/what-are-playstyles)
- FC 26 [official]: "Lob passes will have more driven trajectories when the path is unobstructed." Also: "adjusted Crosses to lead more in front of the target, along with increased accuracy when in easy contexts." The Competitive preset has "Increased variability on crosses, to favour player skill over repetitive wide play". Authentic has "Corner kicks and Crossing tuned to match real-world success rates". — [EA FC 26 Gameplay Deep Dive](https://www.ea.com/games/ea-sports-fc/fc-26/news/pitch-notes-fc26-gameplay-deep-dive)
- FC 26 v1.1.0 (Oct 2025) [secondary report]: passing is "less assisted and more precise"; "Reduced accuracy for headed lobbed passes and crosses"; "Fixed low cross and clearance input delays". — [fut.gg / futwiz summaries](https://www.fut.gg/news/top-10-gameplay-changes-coming-to-ea-sports-fc-26/), [futwiz pitch notes v1.1.0](https://pr-536.deditatedwam.futwiz.com/news/article/fc-26-pitch-notes-v1-1-0/12502) *(snippet)*

**PES / eFootball**
- PES 2019 official manual: the "power gauge will decide how powerful a pass is and how high the ball goes when shooting". — [Konami PES 2019 manual](https://dds.konami.com/games/manual/pes2019/PC/en/control_advance.html) *(snippet)*
- PES lofted pass is ○/B. While the button is held, the stick adjusts trajectory: up = faster, down = slower, left/right = spin. — [PES 2021 controls, realsport101](https://realsport101.com/article/pes-2021-controls-complete-guide-goalkeeper-defence-attack-on-ps4-and-xbox-one-dribbling-passing-shooting-tactics-gameplay-season-update) *(snippet; attributed to official manual)*
- Player skills change lofted balls. "Weighted Pass" adds back-spin; "Low Lofted Pass" gives a long, accurate lofted pass with a low trajectory. — [magicgameworld PES 2021 skills guide](https://guides.magicgameworld.com/?p=11906) *(snippet)*
- eFootball (2022+) [official]: the "Stunning Lofted Pass" is "a lower and faster pass compared to a normal lofted pass" (Classic: R2 + ○ on pad; flick Pass right on mobile). Stunning kicks "take longer to leave the player's feet … such as when stopping or dribbling at low speed". — [Konami eFootball New Controls](https://www.konami.com/efootball/en-us/page/new_controls); pad mapping from [fifplay eFootball controls](https://www.fifplay.com/efootball-2026-controls/) *(snippet)*

**Arcade / older**
- Sensible Soccer: kick height and power come from the **stick direction relative to the player's running direction at the moment of the kick**. Same direction = low, hard; neutral = lofted; pulled back = high lob. Aftertouch then bends the ball in flight. — [Giant Bomb, Sensible Soccer: European Champions](https://www.giantbomb.com/wd/3030-27453) *(snippet)*; aftertouch "a subtle skill to master" — [Pocket Gamer, Sensible Soccer Skillz review](https://www.pocketgamer.com/sensible-soccer-skillz/review/)
- The aftertouch "banana" goes back to MicroProse Soccer (1988, Sensible Software): press fire, then push diagonally to curve. — [fifplay MicroProse Soccer](https://www.fifplay.com/microprose-soccer-gameplay/); [Retro Gamer, history of Sensible Soccer](https://gb.readly.com/magazines/retro-gamer-uk/2023-09-01/64eca5464e8df5a067aa1d3f)

### Inferences
- Across the sims, the convention is **meter = distance/power, and a separate input = trajectory class (normal/high/driven/ground)**. The meter does not set height directly. PES shots are the exception: there the gauge does mean height.
- With assisted passing, the meter mostly **selects which team-mate** gets the lob. The game then solves the trajectory to that player, so hold time affects the outcome only loosely. This is why "lob power inconsistency" complaints come up (see Q4).
- FC 26 picks a flatter trajectory automatically when the lane is clear. That suggests a model where the game chooses the lowest trajectory that clears the obstacles, rather than a fixed arc per button.
- Sensible Soccer shows a cheap 2D alternative: stick-relative-to-motion at the moment of the kick sets the height class, and aftertouch adds curve.

### Gaps
- No official numbers for bar-to-distance or bar-to-height mapping in any EA title. The exact assisted/semi/manual passing behaviour for lobs was not confirmed from an official page (help.ea.com "how to pass" timed out).
- Not confirmed for current FC: early cross, and the exact input for the ground cross (it was □□ in FIFA 20).
- ISS / Winning Eleven (SNES/PS1 era) and Super Sidekicks lob controls: no sources found.

## Q2: Receiving aerial balls (foot/thigh/chest/head selection, input during reception)

### Takeaway
In FIFA/FC the **game picks the body part and animation from context**: ball height, ball speed relative to the player, the requested exit direction, and opponent pressure. The player only shapes it with a direction plus modifiers: hold L2/LT to control better (FC 26), R1 + stick for a set-up touch, R2 + RS for a knock-on, R3/RS for a flick-up or juggle, and L2 + R3 for a chest flick. HyperMotion (FIFA 22+) uses ML on mo-cap data to generate receive and touch animations. The rules that select the animation are not public.

### Cited Findings
**Inputs (FIFA 20 era, official manual transcription)**
- "First touch/ Knock-on: R2 + right stick"; "Disguised first touch: R1 (hold) + left stick (towards ball)"; "Set up touch: R1 + right stick (hold in direction)"; "Flick up for volley: R3" (or "left stick up (hold)" among the juggling tricks); "Chest flick: L2 (hold) + L3 (tap), R3 (triple tap)". The manual has no separate "trap" entry. — [fifauteam FIFA 20/21 manual](https://fifauteam.com/fifa-21-manual/)
- FIFA 19 guide: "Dominate the ball with the chest, knee, or ankle: R3/RS after a long pass". "Soul Trap": hold L2/LT plus right stick forward when the ball is in the air. — via search results ([fifauteam](https://fifauteam.com/?p=54350), [EA FIFA 22 accessibility controls](https://www.ea.com/able/resources/fifa/fifa-22/ps4/basic-controls)) *(snippet; exact source page for each line uncertain)*
- FC 25: "Jostle (Ball in Air): L2/LT"; "Controlled first touch: R1/RB + right stick, flick in any direction or hold". Guides disagree on the flick-up input (R3, or hold left stick up). — [fifauteam FC 25 controls](https://fifauteam.com/fc-25-controls/), [fifplay FC 25 controls](https://www.fifplay.com/fc-25-controls/) *(snippet)*

**Selection logic / system design (official)**
- FIFA 19 "Active Touch System": EA described a rewrite of how the game handles every moment a player touches the ball. It added new first-touch animations, disguised traps, flick-up volleys and signature traps, and some moves depend on player ratings. — [PlayStation Blog, FIFA 19 gameplay changes](https://blog.playstation.com/archive/2018/07/27/how-fifa-19s-biggest-gameplay-changes-make-ea-sports-franchise-more-realistic-than-ever/); [EA FIFA 19 Active Touch](https://www.ea.com/en-gb/games/fifa/news/fifa-19-gameplay-active-touch-system) *(snippet; the EA URL now redirects)*
- FIFA 22 "HyperMotion": Xsens mo-cap of 22 players in full matches, about 8.7M frames. An "ML algorithm" uses it to generate animations "on the fly" for "running, controlling or striking the ball", and the system produced over 4,000 new animations. "Composed Ball Control" adds "longer two-touch animations". This was next-gen only. — [EA FIFA 22 HyperMotion](https://ea.com/games/fifa/fifa-22/hypermotion); [Goal.com](https://www.goal.com/en-gb/news/fifa-22-gameplay-hypermotion-composed-ball-control-new/1jfof69v7he221ds37nkpkh476); [TechRadar](https://techradar.com/news/hypermotion-technology-in-fifa-22-sounds-like-a-game-changer-but-what-is-it)
- **FC 26 [official]**, the most explicit statement of inputs to touch selection: first-touch outcome depends on "ball speed relative to the player's speed, ball height, requested exit angle, opponent pressure". "Holding L2 || LT will greatly improve the players ability to control the ball … Earlier timing of this input will result in increased control." "Holding Sprint prompts immediate acceleration toward the contact point. … Without the Sprint input, the response might be less immediate." New "Shield Trap": "Prioritized getting in the way of incoming opponents instead of getting the perfect touch on the ball." Also "improved flicking actions when receiving an aerial ball in all directions to be more consistent and predictable" and "significantly improved responsiveness of first touch ball control to seamlessly transition into dribbling". — [EA FC 26 Gameplay Deep Dive](https://www.ea.com/games/ea-sports-fc/fc-26/news/pitch-notes-fc26-gameplay-deep-dive)
- FC 26 v1.1.0: "More natural ball trapping and animation transitions." — [futwiz v1.1.0 summary](https://pr-536.deditatedwam.futwiz.com/news/article/fc-26-pitch-notes-v1-1-0/12502) *(snippet)*
- PlayStyle "First Touch" (FC 24+) reduces touch error. Error therefore scales with attributes (Ball Control) and with incoming pace (driven lobs are harder to control). — [Futbin First Touch PlayStyle](https://www.futbin.com/25/playstyles/first-touch); [Dexerto/fifplay on driven lobs](https://www.fifplay.com/fc-27-lob-pass/)

**eFootball / PES**
- eFootball [official]: "Quick Stop / Face the Opponent's Goal" (double-tap on mobile; on Touch & Flick, "double tap the left side when trapping the ball"). "Sharp Touch": a strong first touch with acceleration (pad: fully press R2/RT with the left stick in a direction, then release). — [Konami New Controls](https://www.konami.com/efootball/en-us/page/new_controls); [pesmastery dribbling tutorial](https://pesmastery.com/efootball-passing-tutorial/) *(snippet)*
- In older PES, R2/RT had a long-standing "controlled trap / stop" role. No official page confirming the aerial trap mapping was found (see Gaps).

### Inferences
- The shared model: **the engine picks a contact surface from ball height at the contact point**: foot/sole low, thigh/knee mid, chest high, head above that. The user picks **intent** (direction of exit, keep close versus knock on, flick up, shield) and **commitment** (Sprint to attack the ball; hold L2 early for a cleaner touch). FC 26 rewards early input, so timing is part of the skill: a buffered "control" intent pressed early beats a late one.
- Error is a function of (ball relative speed, height, attribute, pressure, exit angle). This is directly portable to a utility- or learned-model approach.

### Gaps
- No public source gives EA's height thresholds for the chest/thigh/head choice, or describes how HyperMotion/ML picks receive animations at runtime. (GDC talks on FIFA ML locomotion were not located within the search budget.)
- eFootball pad mapping for an aerial "controlled trap" with R2 and chest-down was not confirmed officially.
- "Letting it run" (dummy): no current-era source found in this pass. Older FIFA had a dummy/let-run input (R1 in some eras), which is unconfirmed here.

## Q3: One-touch play off aerial balls (volleys, headed passes, chest-then-X, flick-ons) and input buffering

### Takeaway
In FIFA/FC, **any pass or shot pressed while the ball is still arriving becomes a first-time action using whatever body part fits the ball's height**. Ground pass → headed or volleyed pass, lob → lofted header, shoot → volley or headed shot. FC 24 added explicit "Headers on Demand" (L2 + R2 + pass type) to force a header. Chains (chest then volley) come from flick-up/chest-flick inputs followed by a shot. FC 26 tuned first-time passing to be more responsive but less accurate on Competitive, and made headed lobs less accurate after launch.

### Cited Findings
- FIFA 20 manual: pass, lob and shoot double as header and volley ("Ground pass/ Header", "Lob pass/ Cross/ Header", "Shoot/ Volley/ Header"); "Low shot/ Downward header: L1 + R1 + ○". — [fifauteam manual](https://fifauteam.com/fifa-21-manual/)
- FC 24 "Headers on Demand": L2 + R2 + pass type (LT + RT) makes the player attempt a header "when the ball is above three feet from the ground". It is used for quick header passes, for example to start counters. — [VHPG FC 24 Headers On Demand](https://www.vhpg.com/fc-24-headers-on-demand/) *(community; the 3 ft threshold comes from this one source only)*. EA also added "hundreds of new and refreshed animations for Headers, Jostling Passes, Toe Poke Passes, and Sliding Passes". — [EA FC 24 Pitch Notes (Switch)](https://www.ea.com/games/ea-sports-fc/pitch-notes/news/fc-24-switch-deepdive) *(snippet; the fetched page no longer shows this text)*
- FIFA 23 guide: manual headers generally need the ball at about hip height for shots and about chest height for passes and clearances. — [progameguides FIFA 23 manual headers](https://progameguides.com/fifa/how-to-do-manual-headers-in-fifa-23/) *(snippet, community)*
- FC 26 [official]: "enabled first-touch animations for passes when contextually appropriate, such as short distance passes"; "First-time 135° and 180° through passes are now significantly less accurate"; "in Competitive Gameplay, first-time pass accuracy is reduced, despite being more responsive"; "Increased heading error relative to Authentic" (Competitive); "More realistic header passing and shooting accuracy" (Authentic). Precision Header PlayStyle: "Performs headers with increased accuracy and power". Aerial Fortress: "higher jumps and … physical defensive presence before and during aerial duels"; "Stronger players are able to keep opponents away when fighting for an incoming air ball." Low Driven Volley is listed as an "In Air Shot" mapping. "Improved the Input Processing System." — [EA FC 26 Deep Dive](https://www.ea.com/games/ea-sports-fc/fc-26/news/pitch-notes-fc26-gameplay-deep-dive)
- FC 24 Power Header PlayStyle: "Increased ball speed on Headers, increased accuracy for Headers"; more powerful headers are less likely to sail over. — [EA FC 24 PlayStyles](https://www.ea.com/games/ea-sports-fc/pitch-notes/news/fc-24-playstyles-deepdive)
- FIFA 22 "Kinetic Air Battles": syncs the animations of two players contesting a header and adds pushing, pulling and jostling on crosses, corners, long balls and clearances. — [Sportskeeda FIFA 22 features](https://sportskeeda.com/esports/fifa-22-gameplay-features-revealed-hypermotion-new-ball-control-true-ball-physics)
- Flick-up then volley chain: "Flick up for volley: R3"; chest flick is L2 + R3 combos. — [fifauteam manual](https://fifauteam.com/fifa-21-manual/)
- Sensible Soccer had community-recognised "high/looping headers" off crosses. — [sensiblesoccer.de forum](https://sensiblesoccer.de/forum/amiga/27222-high-looping-headers) *(forum title only)*

### Inferences
- The robust pattern: **buffer the action during the ball's approach, then resolve it at contact**. Which kind of contact (header, volley, chest-then-play) follows from ball height at the contact point, and accuracy is penalised by exit angle relative to the incoming ball (FC 26 nerfs 135°/180° first-time passes).
- EA tunes headed-pass accuracy as a separate knob, per preset and per patch. In real games it is an important balance lever: too accurate and crosses dominate; too inaccurate and "header passes go anywhere".
- Inference only: EA mentions earlier input meaning better control, and gives Sprint as an attack-the-ball modifier. Together these suggest an input buffer that is read during the approach window, not just at contact. The exact frame windows are not public.

### Gaps
- No public frame-window numbers for input buffering on volleys or headers in any title. FC 26 says only "Improved the Input Processing System".
- Exact chest-then-pass behaviour (is a buffered pass executed after a chest touch, or does it become a header?) is not documented officially.
- eFootball header/volley specifics: no official source found.

## Q4: Community complaints and praise (what matters for feel)

### Takeaway
The recurring complaints are: aerial balls not being brought down (the ball flies away or goes to an opponent), the first touch popping the ball up, unwanted flick-ups or lobs from mis-reading input, clunky reception animations, and cross/long-ball inconsistency. EA's own patch direction (FC 26: earlier input means more control, flick consistency, a fix for "low cross input delays") confirms the three feel levers: **predictability, responsiveness, and readable error**.

### Cited Findings
- FC 24 [forum]: "Whenever the ball is in the air, the ball goes flying out of the stadium or into an opponent's foot instead of being brought down." — [EA Forums FC 24 "Players can't take a touch"](https://forums.ea.com/discussions/fc-24-technical-issues-en/players-can%e2%80%99t-take-a-touch-on-eafc24/8023339) *(snippet; page 403)*
- FC 26 [forum]: the ball "bounces very high off the ground on the first touch every time" they receive or turn. — [EA Forums FC 26](https://forums.ea.com/discussions/fc-26-technical-issues-en/fc-26/13007335) *(snippet)*. Reception and first touches called "clumsy and unnatural". — [EA Forums FC 26 feedback](https://forums.ea.com/discussions/fc-26-feedback-en/passing-through-balls-and-ball-control-feel-broken-in-fc-26/13432581) *(snippet)*
- FC 27 [forum/guide]: a ground pass triggers an unwanted "First Touch Flick" or lifts the ball, nicknamed the "Flick Up/Lob glitch". — [EA Forums FC 27 bug](https://forums.ea.com/idea/fc-27-bug-reports-en/ground-passing-non-functional/13716188/comments/13716712); [allthings.how](https://allthings.how/?p=199208) *(snippet)*
- FC 25 [forum]: complaints about first touch on interceptions (the ball deflects away). — [EA Forums FC 25](https://forums.ea.com/discussions/fc-25-general-discussion-en/first-touch-on-interceptions/7991983) *(title only)*
- FC 26 [forum]: a bug-report thread titled "Crosses and long balls". — [EA Forums](https://forums.ea.com/idea/fc-26-bug-reports-en/crosses-and-long-balls/12833645) *(403; content unknown)*
- Career mode: a ball off the chest or leg ricochets onto an arm and a penalty is given immediately. — [EA Forums FC 27](https://forums.ea.com/discussions/fc-27-feedback-en/penalty-decisions/13780347) *(snippet)*
- FIFA 19 beta: Active Touch "can be the reason why you screw up a simple goalscoring opportunity or gift possession". — [WhatCulture](https://whatculture.com/gaming/fifa-19-beta-5-major-problems-that-need-fixing?page=2) *(snippet)*
- FC 26 official response: flicks on aerial balls made "more consistent and predictable"; early L2 hold means more control; "Fixed low cross … input delays" (v1.1.0). — [EA FC 26 Deep Dive](https://www.ea.com/games/ea-sports-fc/fc-26/news/pitch-notes-fc26-gameplay-deep-dive); [futwiz v1.1.0](https://pr-536.deditatedwam.futwiz.com/news/article/fc-26-pitch-notes-v1-1-0/12502)

### Inferences
- Players accept skill-based error, but not error they cannot read. A poor touch should be explainable from visible factors (pace, height, pressure, attribute, awkward exit angle). Random ricochets and unintended flick-ups feel like the game is cheating.
- Overloading the same button (pass = header = volley = flick context) causes "the game did something I didn't press". Context thresholds (for example ball height for header versus kick) need hysteresis and clear visuals.
- Input latency on time-critical aerial actions (crosses, first-time headers) is noticed and complained about. Responsiveness matters as much as accuracy.

### Gaps
- No Reddit threads were retrieved directly. Evidence is from EA forums snippets (many pages return 403 to automated fetch).
- No complaints/praise sources were found for eFootball aerial control, or for arcade-era lob feel.
