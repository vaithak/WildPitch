# Through balls, pass power and pass reception in commercial football games

Evidence notes. "(snippet)" means the claim came from a search-result summary and the page was not fetched in full. EA's main FIFA 21 deep-dive URL returned 404 and the FC 27 deep dive returned 403, so the FIFA 21/FC 27 claims rely on secondary coverage.

## Through balls: power and lead, assistance levels, choosing the target, run triggers, complaints

### Takeaway
In EA FIFA/FC, the through-ball button plus a power bar picks *which* receiver (more power = a teammate further away). The assistance setting decides how much of the direction and power the game corrects so the ball lands "into the receiver's path". The newer games add "passing lanes" (the gap between two defenders) and explicit "lead the receiver" logic. eFootball offers through-ball variants: the "stunning" through ball is faster and finishes closer to the receiver's feet. Run triggering is a separate input, either from the passer (FIFA Directed Pass & Go, eFootball one-two) or as a trigger run. A through ball does not start a run by itself.

### Cited Findings
- FIFA 21 setting "Through Ball Assistance": "decide whether through ball direction and power will be assiste[d] to help play passes into the receiver's path". The default is Assisted. Ground Pass Assistance also says "pass direction and power will be assisted or manual to help play passes into the receiver's path". Lob Pass Assistance: "choose the consistency of lob passes and how strong the pass will be". — [EA Accessibility, FIFA 21 controller settings](https://www.ea.com/able/resources/fifa/fifa-21/xbox-one/controller-settings)
- The same page lists "Pass Receiver Lock": "decide when the pass receiver is locked. The default is set to Late." The FC 24 guide describes a "Power Up" option where the receiver is locked as soon as the button is released or the power bar is full. (FC 24 part is snippet.) — [EA Accessibility FIFA 21](https://www.ea.com/able/resources/fifa/fifa-21/xbox-one/controller-settings); [fifauteam FC 24 settings](https://fifauteam.com/?p=231484)
- Semi-assisted through balls: the game helps only partly with direction and power. Manual: fully manual (snippet). — [fifauteam FC 24 settings](https://fifauteam.com/?p=231484); [ginx FIFA 21 controller tutorial](https://admin.ginx.tv/en/fifa-21/fifa-21-controller-settings-tutorial)
- The power bar mainly selects the receiver. "Holding power for 3 bars makes the game think you want to pass to a player further away than holding it for 1 bar" (FIFA 21 guide, snippet). EA's FIFA Mobile advanced passing notes: "Improved near vs far receiver passing selection" above 50% power for Ground Pass and Lofted Through Pass, and "High Power will now consistently select the further teammate as the receiver." — [dexerto FIFA 21 passing guide](https://dexerto.com/fifa/ultimate-fifa-21-passing-crossing-guide-1407583); [EA Advanced Passing Deep Dive (FIFA Mobile)](https://www.ea.com/able/news/gameplay-advanced-passing)
- Overhitting is the classic mistake: the power "should not be too great, because the player will not be able to reach it" (FIFA 21 guide, snippet). — [gamepressure FIFA 21 passes](https://www.gamepressure.com/fifa-21/passes/zada05)
- FIFA Mobile gesture passing: tapping *ahead* of the receiver triggers a Through Pass. Tapping on or behind him gives a ground pass to feet. So the pass type is inferred from where the target sits relative to the runner. — [EA Advanced Passing Deep Dive](https://www.ea.com/able/news/gameplay-advanced-passing)
- FIFA 21 rebuilt through passing with "better pass targets, receiver selection". "Pass openness" means smart passers draw the receiver away from defenders. "Pass complexity" adds new trajectories and targets so the receiver ends up in a strong position. Through-pass accuracy blends Short/Long Passing, Vision and Composure (secondary, snippet). — [gfinity FIFA 21 features](https://www.gfinityesports.com/article/fifa-21-gameplay-features-player-personality-agile-dribbling-creative-runs-positioning-personality-smother-encounters-ai-ps4-xbox-one-pc); EA original (404): [Pitch Notes FIFA 21 deep dive](https://www.ea.com/news/pitch-notes-fifa-21-gameplay-features-deep-dive)
- FC 26: semi-assisted through passes use "Passing Lanes", "the gap between two opponents", to pick the intended receiver. Through passes "will also now lead receivers more effectively". Receiver selection is improved for ground and through passes. Lob passes take more driven trajectories when the path is unobstructed. First-time 135° and 180° through passes are "significantly less accurate", to stop Directed Run exploits. — [EA FC 26 Pitch Notes deep dive](https://www.ea.com/games/ea-sports-fc/fc-26/news/pitch-notes-fc26-gameplay-deep-dive)
- Run triggering in FIFA 21 "Creative Runs". Directed Runs: press the run button and flick the right stick, 360°. Directed Pass & Go: flick the right stick after any pass (ground, through, lob, cross) to steer the *passer's* follow-up run. Player Lock: click both sticks. Up to 5 players can run at once, and the runs can only be triggered in a limited window (secondary). — [gfinity Creative Runs guide](https://www.gfinityesports.com/article/fifa-21-creative-runs-guide-overview-new-gameplay-off-the-ball-mechanics-controls-tips-skill-gap-changes-and-more)
- FC 26 moved away from triggered runs: "More players make runs, reducing reliance on triggered runs". Runs "more consistently run into open space". Attackers stay onside better. — [EA FC 26 Pitch Notes](https://www.ea.com/games/ea-sports-fc/fc-26/news/pitch-notes-fc26-gameplay-deep-dive)
- FIFA Mobile Trigger Runs: "one of your players to make an attacking run forward" (a direction picks the player, and yellow arrows mark him). "You can trigger up to 4 X runs at once". — [EA Advanced Passing Deep Dive](https://www.ea.com/able/news/gameplay-advanced-passing)
- eFootball (Konami official): the Stunning Through Ball is "faster than a normal Through Ball and tends to end closer to the receiver's feet". The Stunning Chipped Through Ball is pacier, with a slightly lower trajectory, and also ends near the feet. eFootball also adds a pass-and-run "Cross Over", where the passer makes a diagonal run across the receiver. — [Konami eFootball new controls](https://www.konami.com/efootball/ar/page/new_controls)
- eFootball guide: for a normal through ball, aim at a spot in front of the teammate, not at the player. A lofted through ball is L1/LB + through, recommended against crowded defences. Stunning is R2/RT fully pressed: "The pass will travel faster to your recipient". Runs come from the sender, as in the one-two (L1 + low pass, then the passer runs forward). Teammates "gradually return to their original position" after runs. — [PES Mastery eFootball passing tutorial](https://pesmastery.com/efootball-passing-tutorial/)
- Complaints and patches. FIFA 23 TU5 nerfed driven lobbed through balls: "Decreased the accuracy of Driven Lobbed Through Passes aimed away from where the passer is facing" and "Lowered the ball trajectory height of Driven Lobbed Through Passes". TU5 also fixed appropriately powered through passes that could wrongly go out of play (snippet). — [RealSport101 FIFA 23 TU5](https://realsport101.com/article/fifa-23-over-the-top-through-balls-nerfed-title-update-5); [fut.gg TU5](https://www.fut.gg/news/lobbed-through-balls-patched-fifa-23-title-update-5-overview)
- FIFA 23 TU6 fixed defenders that "could incorrectly slow down at times while chasing lofted through passes" (snippet). FIFA Mobile fixed lobbed through passes over the top that left the receiver "stuck in Attacking control state, so they couldn't rush the GK", and some "extreme trajectory errors" on lobbed and ground through passes. — [fut.gg TU6](https://www.fut.gg/news/ea-patch-pressing-fifa-23-title-update-6-overview); [EA Advanced Passing Deep Dive](https://www.ea.com/able/news/gameplay-advanced-passing)
- FC 25 (Jan 2025 update) fixed free-kick passes that were not hit toward the intended receiver "if that receiver was moving too quickly" (snippet). This points to target-prediction failures with fast runners. — [Dexerto FC 25 Jan 15 patch](https://dexerto.com/ea-sports-fc/ea-fc-25-jan-15-update-patch-notes-faster-passing-goalkeeper-shooting-buffs-3031894)

### Inferences
- The common design pattern: input picks a *receiver* (stick direction plus power as a near/far selector), then the assisted solver picks the *target point and speed* in that receiver's path. Manual settings expose raw direction and power. On assisted, the power bar works more like a receiver selector than a distance control.
- The "stunning"/driven variant is useful mainly because it trades lead distance for speed: the ball arrives sooner, closer to the feet, and is harder to intercept.
- Patch history shows the hard cases: passes aimed far from the passer's facing direction, lofted balls over the top that are too strong, and fast-moving receivers breaking the prediction. Each was handled by an accuracy penalty or a trajectory clamp, not by removing the pass.

### Gaps
- No primary source gave numeric lead distances, bar-to-metres mappings or ball speeds for FIFA/FC or eFootball through balls.
- I found no patch note that addresses the receiver slowing down or stopping to collect a through ball.
- Rematch through-ball specifics were not found. Football Manager 3D was not researched within the budget.

## Regular passes: power, assisted speed, pass error, ground/driven/lob speeds, trap error

### Takeaway
Assisted passing in FIFA scales the pass to the receiver's distance and path. Driven passes add "trap error" that grows with pass distance and speed and shrinks with the receiver's Ball Control. In Rematch and eFootball, a longer hold means a stronger pass. Short, low-power passes get fast animations. Kick Off and Sensible Soccer show that simpler models (a cone widened by the passing skill, the ball not stuck to the feet) can carry the feel.

### Cited Findings
- FIFA Mobile: Driven Ground Passes "add trap error based on pass distance, speed, and the receiver's Ball Control attribute". Driven Lob Passes have "higher receiver trap error, based on distance and speed combined with the receiver's Ball Control". Driven Lob is "Lob Passes with more pace and power" with a lower trajectory. Lob passes are "now fully assisted". — [EA Advanced Passing Deep Dive](https://www.ea.com/able/news/gameplay-advanced-passing)
- FC 26: short, low-power passes favour faster animations with minimal wind-up. First-touch pass animations are enabled "when contextually appropriate, such as short distance passes". Competitive mode has "Increased passing speed and responsiveness", but first-time pass accuracy is reduced there. Ground passes have a straighter trajectory with less curve, and teammates hold position to receive them. — [EA FC 26 Pitch Notes](https://www.ea.com/games/ea-sports-fc/fc-26/news/pitch-notes-fc26-gameplay-deep-dive)
- FIFA 21 Semi ground passing: the game assists the power, but direction only slightly (snippet). — [ginx FIFA 21 controller tutorial](https://admin.ginx.tv/en/fifa-21/fifa-21-controller-settings-tutorial)
- eFootball uses a 1–4 "passing assistance" scale (default 3). Level 1 gives a high level of AI assistance, and level 4 gives the least. The stunning pass takes longer to leave the feet when kicked from a stop or a slow dribble. — [PES Mastery passing tutorial](https://pesmastery.com/efootball-passing-tutorial/); [Konami new controls](https://www.konami.com/efootball/ar/page/new_controls)
- Rematch: the pass aims 360° with the left stick. A quick tap gives a softer pass, and holding adds power "to break lines or send it deep". Lob is a separate modified input (RB+X / R1+Square on controller per some guides). Sources disagree on the bindings (snippet). — [Destructoid](https://www.destructoid.com/how-to-pass-the-ball-in-rematch/); [GamePressure](https://www.gamepressure.com/newsroom/how-to-pass-in-rematch-learn-it-before-beta-ends/z77f8b); [Deltia's](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=207427)
- Sensible Soccer (Jon Hare): "Passing skill opens up the length of the pass and also the width of the passing cone... meaning more players can be found." — [SWOS United Q&A with Jon Hare](https://www.swosunited.com/forum/swos-related/24921-i-bring-you-the-word-of-go-ehm-mr-jon-hare-gameplay-mechanics-q-amp-a-session)
- Sensible Soccer has aftertouch: a stick input just after the kick bends the ball. Hare traces the idea back through MicroProse Soccer to Tehkan World Cup (snippet). — [Hexus Jon Hare interview](https://hexus.net/gaming/features/industry/4529-sensible-soccer-jon-hare-interview/?page=3)
- Kick Off: the ball "did not stick to the player's feet but instead was realistically kicked ahead". This added difficulty and a skill requirement (snippet). — [Wikipedia, Kick Off series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kick_Off_(series))

### Inferences
- A cheap, robust model for a small game: assisted pass speed = f(distance to the predicted reception point), clamped to a per-type range (ground, driven, lob). Trap error ∝ (ball speed relative to the receiver) × (1 − control skill). That matches FIFA's stated factors.
- SWOS-style "passing skill = cone width + max length" gives receiver selection that scales with skill, without any hidden solver.

### Gaps
- No concrete ball speeds (m/s) or power-bar curves were found for any title.

## Reception: receiver behaviour during flight, control switching, stick-directed first touch, touch quality

### Takeaway
Modern games let the stick input made *before or at* reception decide the first touch. eFootball: the left stick sets the facing direction after the trap. FIFA/FC: R1 + right stick gives a controlled touch, and R2 + right-stick flick gives a big knock-on. FC 26: holding LT gives a more precise touch at the cost of speed, and the earlier the input, the more control. Touch difficulty rises with ball speed, ball height, pressure and attributes. FIFA defaults to switching control on air and loose balls, not on every pass.

### Cited Findings
- FC 26 ball control after a pass: difficulty depends on "ball speed, height, pressure, body part used", plus Ball Control, Interceptions and Composure. First-touch responsiveness was improved so players move straight into dribbling. Holding L2/LT improves control at the cost of movement speed, and "Earlier input gives more control". A Shield Trap (LT while shielding) favours blocking the opponent over a perfect touch, with Strength as a factor. — [EA FC 26 Pitch Notes](https://www.ea.com/games/ea-sports-fc/fc-26/news/pitch-notes-fc26-gameplay-deep-dive)
- FC 26 guide. Aggressive first touch / knock-on: hold RT *before the pass arrives*, then flick the right stick twice toward the target on reception, holding the second flick, to push the ball into space for a sprint. Controlled first touch: hold RB as the ball arrives and push the right stick toward the touch direction, for small touches in tight areas. — [MagicGameWorld FC 26 first touch](https://www.magicgameworld.com/?p=129047)
- FIFA/FC control tables list "First Touch/Knock-On" as R2 + right stick, and "Let Ball Run" / "Disguised First Touch" as R1 + left stick (snippet). — [RealSport101 FC 25 controls](https://www.realsport101.com/article/fc-25-controls-guide)
- FIFA 19 "Active Touch System" was "a rewrite of how the game calculates every moment in which a player touches the ball", with trapping, feints and flicks. You can feint one way before receiving and touch the ball the other way. It was motivated by long-standing complaints about unrealistic first touches since FIFA 13's First Touch Control. Reviews: first touches "much more unpredictable", and sometimes it ruins simple chances (snippet). In FIFA 20 coverage, players "don't slow down to take a touch" (snippet). — [EA FIFA 19 Active Touch](https://www.ea.com/en-gb/games/fifa/news/fifa-19-gameplay-active-touch-system) (fetch returned an unrelated page); [Twinfinite](https://twinfinite.net/features/ea-finally-getting-fix-fifa/); [T3 review](https://t3.com/reviews/fifa-19); [Operation Sports FIFA 20](https://www.operationsports.com/fifa-20-pitch-notes-breakdown)
- eFootball receiving: "Use the left analog stick to direct the position you want your player to face after receiving the ball. Choose to turn away from the marker, or get ready to sprint off in another direction." Trap feint: release the left stick and push the right stick toward where the ball is heading. Pressing the through button before the receiver gets the ball makes him play a first-time return (one-two). — [PES Mastery passing tutorial](https://pesmastery.com/efootball-passing-tutorial/)
- eFootball "Quick Stop / Face the Opponent's Goal": double tap when trapping (mobile). "Sharp Touch" is a strong touch followed by acceleration (Classic: flick while pressing Dash). — [Konami new controls](https://www.konami.com/efootball/ar/page/new_controls)
- PES 2017 Real Touch: the ways a player receives the ball depend on "the height and power of the pass, and the natural ability of the player". PES 2018 Real Touch+ added chest, head and legs, chosen by height and pace (snippet, Konami marketing). — [Konami PES topics](https://www.konami.com/games/eu/en/topics/13633/); [GamingBolt](https://gamingbolt.com/15-things-you-need-to-know-before-you-buy-pes-2018-and-fifa-18/13)
- Sensible Soccer: ball control got harder the faster you ran (snippet). Hare: "in general the ball should not get further away from the player as he runs." — [Everand history of Sensible Soccer](https://www.everand.com/article/669016489/The-History-Of-Sensible-Soccer); [SWOS United Q&A](https://www.swosunited.com/forum/swos-related/24921-i-bring-you-the-word-of-go-ehm-mr-jon-hare-gameplay-mechanics-q-amp-a-session)
- FIFA Auto Switching options. FIFA 21: Air Balls and Loose Balls / Auto / Manual (EA's accessibility page lists the default as Manual). FIFA 22: Manual, Only Loose Balls, Only Air Balls, Automatic, On Air Balls and Loose Balls, default "On Air Balls and Loose Balls" (snippet). One guide says air balls means a ball 8 ft or higher (snippet). — [EA Accessibility FIFA 21](https://www.ea.com/able/resources/fifa/fifa-21/xbox-one/controller-settings); [EA Accessibility FIFA 22](https://www.ea.com/able/resources/fifa/fifa-22/ps5/customise-controls-settings); [fifauteam FIFA 23](https://fifauteam.com/fifa-23-controller-settings/)
- FIFA Mobile fix: after lobbed through passes the receiver was left "stuck in Attacking control state", unable to rush the GK. This shows that the receiver's control-state transition after a long ball is a real bug class. — [EA Advanced Passing Deep Dive](https://www.ea.com/able/news/gameplay-advanced-passing)

### Inferences
- The first touch is decided by an input *buffered before arrival*: eFootball's left-stick facing, FIFA's RT/RB held before the ball arrives, FC 26's "earlier input gives more control". A small game can sample the stick in a window before contact and use it as the touch direction.
- A fast ball relative to the receiver plus high speed or pressure produces a heavy touch. This shows up in FIFA (stated factors), SWOS (harder at speed) and PES (pass power and height).

### Gaps
- No source states exactly what the FIFA/eFootball receiver AI does during the flight of a through ball (keeps running vs decelerates). I only found indirect evidence (FC 26 "teammates hold position" for ground passes; FIFA 20 "don't slow down to take a touch").
- No Rematch first-touch mechanics were found. Reviews only say the timing is strict.

## Stick hand-off: stopping the new player running away or backwards after a switch

### Takeaway
I found one explicit commercial mechanism: FIFA's "Auto Switching Move Assistance" (None/Low/Medium/High) keeps the auto-switched player moving in his current direction for a short time. This hides stick carry-over. Sensible Soccer instead uses the held stick direction to *choose* the next player.

### Cited Findings
- FIFA 21: "Auto Switching Move Assistance: Select between 'None,' 'Low,' 'Medium,' and 'High' to keep the player you AUTO switch to moving in his current direction". The FIFA 21 accessibility page lists the default as None. — [EA Accessibility FIFA 21](https://www.ea.com/able/resources/fifa/fifa-21/xbox-one/controller-settings)
- An August 2022 EA gameplay note says this assistance is enabled regardless of the switching setting (snippet; the page now redirects). — careers.ea.com/games/fifa/news/gameplay-improvements-august-2022 (dead link, via search snippet)
- Sensible Soccer: "Next player selection is made based on the direction that you are pressing the joystick in..." and "not selecting a direction at all is certainly not good to speed up player selection." — [SWOS United Q&A](https://www.swosunited.com/forum/swos-related/24921-i-bring-you-the-word-of-go-ehm-mr-jon-hare-gameplay-mechanics-q-amp-a-session)
- FIFA "Pass Receiver Lock" (default Late) controls when the receiver is committed, which affects when control can pass to him. — [EA Accessibility FIFA 21](https://www.ea.com/able/resources/fifa/fifa-21/xbox-one/controller-settings)

### Inferences
- A practical recipe from the above: after a switch, keep the receiver's AI/current velocity for a short blend window (FIFA's "Low/Medium/High" implies a tunable duration), or until the stick input changes noticeably from what was held at hand-off. Then hand full control to the stick.

### Gaps
- No public numbers for the hand-off delay or blend durations, and no GDC talk on this was found.
